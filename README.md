# Retail_Sales_EDA

This is an exploratory Data Analysis of Retail sales data. 
The goal is to clean, analyse and gather insights from the data.
Used Pandas and Numpy libraries to perform the task.

# Insights from the Analysis
1) Investigate why East has the lowest margin at 18.86% before pursuing additional growth.
2) Online and Store have almost identical margins (~21%), so channel strategy should consider Sales, AOV, customer behavior and growth—not margin alone.
3) High Value customers generate somewhat higher revenue per customer, but their margin is lower. Target Regular customers with potential to increase frequency/AOV      while protecting margin.
4) The top 10 customers generate 26.6% of Sales while representing only 12.8% of customers. Developing the next customer tier can broaden the revenue base.
